```
MapleAlerts is the only app built specifically for Canadian finances.

🍁 FIND YOUR MONEY
See exactly how much unused TFSA and RRSP contribution room you have — and what it's worth in tax savings. CCB estimates, carbon rebate amounts, OAS clawback warnings. Real dollar amounts, not vague advice.

🛡️ PENALTY GUARDRAILS
Get warned before CRA penalizes you. TFSA over-contribution warning before the 1%/month penalty hits. RRSP buffer tracker. "MapleAlerts just saved me $840" — moments like this get shared.

📅 EVERY CANADIAN DEADLINE
RRSP deadline, TFSA room reset, tax filing, GST/HST instalments, CCB dates, Bank of Canada rate decisions, FHSA contribution deadline, HBP repayments. All free. All with timely reminders.

💰 BEST RATES, CONTEXTUAL
See what high-interest savings accounts are actually paying (EQ Bank, Wealthsimple, Neo, and more). When your GIC matures, we show you the best current rate — don't auto-renew at a lower rate.

🎯 YOUR BEST MOVE RIGHT NOW
Cross-account recommendations that siloed tools never make: "GIC matures in 18 days + $14k TFSA room → shelter it and save $568 in tax." One clear action, not a list of options.

✅ FREE. PRIVATE. YOURS.
No bank connection required. No account needed. Your data stays on your phone. Works offline. The math is done on your device using official CRA rules.

⭐ GO PRO ($2.99/month)
Partner/spouse account tracking, contribution pace tracker, net worth history, PDF year summary for your accountant, custom reminder timing. Every penny of your Canadian financial picture.
```
